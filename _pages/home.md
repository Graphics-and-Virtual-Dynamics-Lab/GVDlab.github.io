The **Graphics and Virtual Dynamics Lab** is a research group in Computer Science within the [Faculty of Science](https://science.ontariotechu.ca/index.php) at [Ontario Tech University](https://ontariotechu.ca/).

Our research explores the intersection of **virtual characters**, **human-building interaction**, **serious games**, and **immersive crowds and environments**. We develop computational models that simulate complex human behavior and crowd dynamics, with applications in interactive worlds, smart architectural design, emergency evacuation planning, and other virtual environments.

Through **virtual reality**, **embodied interaction**, and **3D digital twins**, we study how people perceive and navigate built environments. Our work aims to connect research insights with practical tools for safer, more believable, and more responsive digital spaces.
