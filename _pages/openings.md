<!-- POSTDOCTORAL POSITIONS -->

<article class="gvd-position-card">

<span class="gvd-label">Postdoctoral research</span>

<h3>Postdoc Positions</h3>

<p>We are excited to welcome talented postdoctoral researchers who are ready to take their careers to the next level in the fields of crowd simulation, virtual reality, and emotionally-aware virtual environments.</p>

<p>To express your interest, please send us an email with the subject line <strong>Application for Postdoc</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>Application materials:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Curriculum vitae (CV).</li>
<li>Academic transcripts.</li>
<li>A brief description of the research project you are excited to work on.</li>
</ul>

<p>We especially encourage postdocs with fellowships. Consider exploring funding opportunities such as:</p>

<ul>
<li><a href="https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PD-NP/cpra-bprc_eng.asp">NSERC Postdoctoral Research Award</a>.</li>
<li><a href="https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html">Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship information</a>.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Be mindful of application deadlines and confirm current program availability.</strong> International applicants should also explore fellowships available in their home countries for outbound postdoctoral researchers.</p>

<article class="gvd-position-card">

<span class="gvd-label">Graduate studies</span>

<h3>Master's and PhD Positions</h3>

<p>Thinking of graduate studies with a funded opportunity? Our graduate students are supported through Research Assistantships and/or scholarships.</p>

<p>While admission is competitive, you significantly boost your chances by securing external funding or scholarships.</p>

<p>Interested in joining us? Send an email with the subject line <strong>Graduate Admission</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>Application materials:</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Curriculum vitae (CV).</li>
<li>Academic transcripts.</li>
<li>A brief outline of a potential research project you are excited about.</li>
</ul>

<div class="gvd-note">

<p><strong>Important:</strong> Admissions are managed by Graduate Studies at Ontario Tech University. Applicants must follow the university's formal admission process.</p>

</div>

<p style="margin-top:20px;"><strong>Graduate program information:</strong></p>
